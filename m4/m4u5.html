Unit 5

Inital post:

The article (Gu et al, 2023) points out the use of aesthetic bias against software, with ‘prettier’ software being perceived more highly by users. This is something that fades over time but the bias is still there. This is something that I feel is definitely present as people tend to trust software that fits a certain perceived aesthetic more than software’s that don’t. I feel when it comes to subjective evaluation it is difficult to improve its accuracy as these areas are so subjective and dependant on so many factors. I feel it could be more standardised but again this could be tricky as there are so many external factors that can influence the results. For example, the websites visited by the participants in their own time could influence how they view the test webpages and there usability.

References:

  • Gu, Q., Tang, W., Xue, C. (2023) ‘The Effect of Time Lapse on the Halo Effect in the Subjective Evaluation of Digital Interfaces.’, Design, User Experience, and Usability, 14032. Available at: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-35702-2_12
