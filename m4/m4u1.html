Unit 1

Unit 1 Discussion topic: Question 1

Based on the research (Agrawal et al. 2024), I'd say the three main reasons that projects fail are: Not knowing the business: When designers don't understand the business and how it works, designs can become flawed. This can cause fundamental areas to be missed. Such as software that just doesn't do what users need. Organisational problems: Things like time pressure, not enough resources, bad communication, and lack of training causing designer to mess up designs. Sometimes the company's issues stop designers from doing their best work. Designer slip-ups: Stress, being tired, or just not paying attention can cause mistakes. They might be small, but they still cause problems.

Unit 1 Discussion topic: Question 2

Some examples of failures from the article (Agrawal et al. 2024) are: Not knowing the business: The article (Agrawal et al. 2024), mentions a case where a software designer's lack of understanding of order management led to errors in the design. This caused the system to not be able to determine the status of orders. Organisational problems: the article (Agrawal et al. 2024), mentions that deadlines made designers skip updating password their passwords, which made the system less secure. This would make the system more vulnerable to cyber-attacks.

References:

Agrawal, T., Walia, G. S. & Anu, V. K. (2024) Development of a Software Design Error Taxonomy: A Systematic Literature Review. SN Computer Science 5(467): 1-21.
