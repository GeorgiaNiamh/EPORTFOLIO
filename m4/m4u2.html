Unit 2: Peer response

From reading the responses of my colleagues to the article (Agrawal et al. 2024), I think that there was a lot of cross over between the ideas as to why projects may fail. For example, the idea of unrealistic goals being set (Williams, 2025), I feel is a key reason that can cause project failure. In terms of mitigating this issue I feel a clear and precise planning phase is needed to ensure that the goals set are feasible for all involved. This plan should take into account the different roles and responsibilities of key groups working on the project. I also feel the point raised by Gore (2025) about communication breakdowns causing failures to be interesting. This issue could be mitigated by making sure there are compulsory meeting times where progress can be discussed to ensure that communication remains robust. Gore (2025) mentions an example of one of these communication failures with the Challenger Space Shuttle Disaster of 1986. Particularly when it came to designers and engineers miscommunicating an issue to their higher ups (Harris, 1992). Again I feel this could have been mitigated with clearer lines of communication between higher ups and workers this could have prevented the issue from going unnoticed.


References:
  • Agrawal, T., Walia, G. S. & Anu, V. K. (2024) Development of a Software Design Error Taxonomy: A Systematic Literature Review. SN Computer Science 5(467): 1-21.
  • Gore, S. (2025) ‘Top 3 Reasons for Project Failures’, Collaborative Discussion 1: Project Failures Study, in SEPM_PCOM7E: Software Engineering Project Management.
