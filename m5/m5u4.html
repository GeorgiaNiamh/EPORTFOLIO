Unit 4

Literature review summary:



The literature review is taking shape in the format according to the plan I have carried out reading around the topics for each section. I decided for 3 distinct sections as I the research around AI and animals has many forms and I felt it was best to categorise them. The review does not contain much information on synthetic data even though from my reading I determined these areas was expanding due to restraints in terms of the word count. Below is a summary of my section and reading associated:

Section 1: Classification through machine learning

Looks into different Ai tools such as CNNs, SVMs and K-NNs (positives and negatives for each). Discusses how limited data sets creates issues for training and implementation.

Readings:
Gorai, S., Sarangi, A., Pradhan, S. (2025). ‘Deep Learning for Image Classification: Methods, Challenges, and Future Directions’ International Journal of Scientific Research in Computer Science Engineering and Information Technology, 11(1):484-496 DOI: 10.32628/CSEIT2511110
Kimly, Y., Lany, M., Vitou, S., Kor, A. (2023). ‘Animal Classification using Convolutional Neural Network’. The 2nd Student Conference on Digital Technology 2023. Cambodia, December 2023. Cambodia, Cambodia Academy of Digital Technology. Available at: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/376751387_Animal_Classification_using_C onvolutional_Neural_Network
Tabak, MA., Norouzzadeh, MS., Wolfson, DW., et al. (2019) ‘Machine learning to classify animal species in camera trap images: Applications in ecology.’ Methods Ecol Evol. 2019; 10: 585–590. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210X.13120

Section 2: Detection and segementation of animal images

Looks into how segmentation can be used with bounding boxes along with how the quality of data can impact this training. Also mentions how synthetic data can assist with these issues.

Readings:
Antônio, W. H. S., Da Silva, M., Miani, R. S., & Souza, J. R. (2019). ‘A Proposal of an Animal Detection System Using Machine Learning.’ Applied Artificial Intelligence, 33(13), 1093–1106. https://doi.org/10.1080/08839514.2019.1673993
Kapoor, V. (2024). Key differences between object detection and image segmentation. Available at: https://mindkosh.com/blog/key-differences-between-object-detection and-image-segmentation/
Kumar, S., Manohar, N., Chethan, H. (2015) ‘Animal Classification System: A Block Based Approach’, Procedia Computer Science, 45, 336-343. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.procs.2015.03.156.

Section 3: Developments in animal pose and action recognition

This section looks at the development of new techniques for recognising behaviour and movements it mainly currently focuses on mice as this seems to be an animal that has looks of available data to analyse.

Readings:
Fuchs, M., Genty, E., Zuberbühler, K., Cotofrei, P. (2023). ‘ASBAR: an Animal Skeleton Based Action Recognition framework. Recognizing great ape behaviors in the wild using pose estimation’ BioRxiv. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1101/2023.09.24.559236
