Reflection:



For this reflection I shall reflect on several areas. Firstly, I shall reflect on my knowledge gained through lectures, seminars and class discussions. Secondly, I shall reflect on my knowledge gained through the assignments of the literature review and the project proposal presentation. Finally, I shall reflect on how the module has enhanced my skills in all aspects of my progression through the master’s degree.
When it comes to research methods and professional practice prior to this module my knowledge was ambiguous at best. I had never carried out formal research within a computer related field before, and the methodological structure was very different to the research I had completed during my undergraduate degree. This led to a steep learning curve for me to overcome when it came to independent work.
This was initially evident when I came to the discussion surrounding ethics in computing. As I do not currently work in the field my knowledge of industry ethics was largely surface level. I managed to significantly increase my knowledge through the reading of the BCS (2022) code of conduct and the ACM code of ethics (2018). These two documents allowed me to build a strong foundation around ethical principles which was then solidified through the case study review tasks. These tasks showed me that being a professional in computing requires many areas beyond just ethics to be review such as laws (GDPR, 2016) and the social impact a lack of ethics can result in (ACAS, 2024). Applying these ideas to the AI ethics task was a key moment, as it allowed me to evaluate critically how difficult it is to stick to these professional standards in the fast-moving world of modern technology.
Another area of growth was my ability to process, understand and represent data in a meaningful way. With the activities within the module, I think my proficiency in this area is vastly improved. I have transitioned from a novice to someone that is now confident in these areas due to my engagement with the worksheet tasks (as seen in the unit 7, 8 and 9 sections of my e-portfolio). A key insight was realising the importance of selecting the appropriate tests based on data type, rather than just running calculations. This understanding is vital for ensuring the validity and reliability of any future research findings. I know now that I have acquired this skill, I can utilise it within my dissertation and within my work outside of the course in professional practice.

Secondly, I shall reflect on my work for the literature review and the research proposal. I was never required to complete a literature review prior to this module causing me to doubt my abilities. While I began the module with little understanding of what a literature review should contain, the module enabled my understanding to flourish. I worked hard to plan and read broadly around my chosen topic, which then resulted in me dividing the literature into sections that reflected the recurring themes. Overall, I critically evaluated my literature review and know it could have been more comprehensive in its analysis. The feedback I received was very helpful in increasing my understanding of the aims and content of any future literature reviews I may write. If I were to attempt the submission again, I feel I would approach it in a much more organised and focused manner than in this module. I hope this would translate into an improvement in the quality of the review. This experience was fundamental in building my confidence and providing the skills to improve this area going forward.
The second assignment for the research proposal also took me outside of my comfort zone, as I had only completed one research proposal previously in a vastly different field. The concepts discussed in seminars and lectures around data gathering and methodology selection were fundamental in assisting me. I started in a place of confusion surrounding the task, which was rapidly eased by the structured work in the module.
When it came to the feedback I received, I was grateful for the clarity in identifying areas for my improvement, specifically the importance of justifying my choices and analysing potential dataset bias. This feedback has assisted me in understanding the rigor needed within any future research proposals I may complete especially when it comes to the areas surrounding ethics.

Finally, I shall critically review my overall progress according to the skills matrix and SWOT I completed in unit 11 (see unit 11 of the e-portfolio for a simplified version). Through this evaluation, I can see how my abilities and skills have transformed. In the skills matrix many areas moved from ‘aware’ to ‘trained’ with some skills getting to the point where I feel proficient.
I enjoyed reflecting on the way my skills have developed, along with focusing on how this and other modules have contributed to these improvements across the key skill areas. In particular I feel the areas of ethical awareness and decision making were vastly improved during this module due to the nature of the tasks I completed allowing me to apply theory to practice.
The SWOT allowed me to think and focus on areas to improve and how to get there, along with any challenges I feel I may face. I focused on my research skills based on the feedback from the assignments and how the feedback could be applied to improve my skills. Using the SWOT in this way has given me clear goals to focus on going forward to further my development.
I feel this module has provided me an essential foundation for independent work going forward. It has substantially improved my confidence in data analysis and critical writing, directly addressing key requirements for the upcoming dissertation module. By facing and critically evaluating the challenges that come with formal research design and methodology, I now feel prepared and ready to embark on the final project.
