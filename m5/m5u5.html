Case study: inappropriate use of surveys


The Cambridge Analytica scandal in 2018 involved the use of surveys as a means to gather information from users through their Facebook accounts though the guise of a personality quiz (Cadwalladr, 2018). The quiz was used to unethically gather large quantities of information on Facebook users. This data was then used to target political advertising to users during the 2016 Us election and UK EU referendum (Cloud, 2023). This use of information went against GDPR (2016) laws and resulted in a loss of trust for many Facebook users.
Another example similar was brought to light in 2012 by New York Times writer Charles Duhigg, He uncovered that target the US retailer had been using consumer data to predict if female customers were pregnant and using this insight to push certain products to them. Although did not violate US law at the time it still raised ethical concerns as many saw the targeting as an invasion of privacy and made users question what else was being monitored eroding trust.

References:

Questionnaire analysis:


NEU bullying questionnaire (Clay, 2019) analysis and review.
The survey is designed to be used in a single setting among a group of people it seems to be designed to be used to survey staff in a single school setting giving it a collective purpose. The strengths are that the survey indicates a clear time frame from which the surveyed have to recall information from. This allows the survey to not be too broad in scope and have a clear focus. The questions are clear and have a yes/no format meaning that there will be no ambiguity from those filling the questionnaire out. I will now discuss specific questions.
Question 1: Have you, in the last three years, had any symptoms which you attribute to stress at work which resulted in your absence from work?
Good clear question with 2 possible answers of Yes or No. To improve the question, it could have asked for how many days to build a better picture as the amount of days could help indicate the level of bullying.
Question 3: this directly links to question 1 and asks the surveyed to If you answered YES to either of the questions above, do you attribute your stress to any of the following? (Please tick all that apply). Followed by a list of issues. The issues outlined are very vague and could be interpreted differently by different people for example one is management style, but this could be linked to many different people and areas within a school setting. Overall, I think each area needed to have an example.
Overall, I think the survey works as an overview but could be improved with added detail on certain questions.

Reference:
Clay, L. (2019) Bullying questionnaire- Members collective questionnaire on bullying. Available at: https://neu.org.uk/latest/library/bullying-questionnaire
