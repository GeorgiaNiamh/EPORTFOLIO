Unit 2

Literature review inital plan:


Computer vision for animals



• Animals are novel as the data can be scarce
• Animals can be studied the focus of many computing problems.
• Segmentation with bounding boxes used to pick up animals from backgrounds
• Not all animals are represented in the data and studies
• Mice are a key focus of the studies in AI for animals due to their prevalence within lab settings

Sections plan



Section 1: classification of images
Easier with less types of animals. Involves segmentation. I Naturalist a website that holds data on many types of animals.
Section 2: Detection and segmentation
Improving due to new technology coming in. (transformers, YOLO and Mask RCNN). Lack of data on animals can be a problem with these. Some animals are more popular/niche compared to others.
Section 3: Novel problems/ new ideas
Pose/action recognition, behaviour monitoring/interpretation. 3D reconstruction. Use of AI to create data for niche animals.
